Complete exploratory extension on previously used TEST data; outside the original Holm family.
Scope: 16 recording dates, 32 prespecified rotated windows, 5 original training seeds, 160 cases, 800 trajectories, 480 seed/window/deviator pairs and 76,800 scored search decisions. No retraining or packet simulation.

Limitations:
  - Exploratory extension on reused TEST data; no Holm-adjusted or confirmatory claims.
  - Date/window confidence intervals condition on the five fitted networks; seed spread is separate.
  - Traffic/channel capture reuse can create dependence beyond Puffer dates.
  - Unconstrained best response is a numerical search lower bound, not a certified global maximum.
  - This experiment does not test Protected deviations or rerun packet simulation.
